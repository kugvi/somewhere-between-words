I recently finished reading "Im Meer waren wir nie" by Meral Kureyshi, and somehow the story has stayed with me. Especially the way she writes. Her language is so poetic, yet simple. Sometimes just a few words or a short sentence can say so much. I love how she manages to capture feelings that are difficult to put into words. It's one of those books that stays with you long after you've finished reading it.

Anonym, 29/10/26, Geneva
