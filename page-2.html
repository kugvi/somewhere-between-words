I moved to another country just as summer was coming to an end. At first, everything felt new, and I was too busy getting used to the unfamiliar streets and faces to notice how much time was passing. Then one morning, I noticed the leaves turning yellow. Some had already fallen onto the pavement, and the air had that particular coldness that always reminds me of October at home. I thought about the trees outside my old window, the familiar walks, and how everything there must be changing too, only this time without me. I wondered whether the leaves had already started falling or if the mornings were getting colder. It was strange to think that autumn was happening in two places at once, and that for the first time, I was only there to see one of them.

Anonym, 28/10/26, Geneva
