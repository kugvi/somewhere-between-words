somewhere between words

Hi, this is an online platforme to share your poems and write stories together! An ever-changing collection of anonymous stories, thoughts and poems, written by strangers from everywhere. Choose an image to begin. Follow the stories wherever they take you. Leave a piece of yourself for someone else to find. No beginning, no ending, no particular order. Just words passing from one stranger to another. Write something on the left. A story, a memory, a thought, anything you wish to share. Tell me where in the world you're writing from, and let your words travel.
