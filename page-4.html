Night is filled with colors more vivid and richer than the day. In a room of 22m², I cut my hair without thinking, I turn the bedroom upside down, and I put on bold makeup that I would not normally allow myself. The world outside the window seems distant and deserted, as if there were no human presence at all. Reason and control are the most important virtues in my life. But in this moment, the desire I had suppressed explodes. At dawn, I let go of control, I become more honest with my desires, and I wander. I wonder whether these unstable moments might not be the truest ones. On the days when I want to share this state with someone, I talk with my friends over video call. In everyday life, when we are together in person, certain stories are hard to tell. But at dawn, they come out naturally. A call that begins at 11 p.m. goes on until 2 a.m. Each of us is in a different space, but we feel that time is infinite. I am drawn to this incomplete, unstable state. It creates a feeling of comfort. However, the sun rises quickly. The noise and the light return, and I hide my true nature once more behind an appearance of normality.

Anonymous, 30/10/26, Geneva
